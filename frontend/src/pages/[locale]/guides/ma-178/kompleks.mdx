## <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.title' />

<Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.intro' block><MathBlock slot="z1" tex="z_1=a+bi" /><MathBlock slot="z2" tex="z_2=c+di" /></Translate>

- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.addition' /> $z_1+z_2 = (a+c) + (b+d)\,i$
- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.subtraction' /> $z_1-z_2 = (a-c) + (b-d)\,i$
- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.multiplication' /> $z_1 \cdot z_2 = (ac-bd) + (ad+bc)\,i$
- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.conjugate' /> $\overline{z_1} = a-b\,i$
- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='rules.absolute' /> $\lvert z_1 \rvert=\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$

### <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='division.title' />

<Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='division.intro' block />

$$
\frac{a+b\,i}{c+d\,i} = \frac{(a+b\,i)(c-d\,i)}{(c+d\,i)(c-d\,i)} = \frac{(a+b\,i)(c-d\,i)}{c^2+d^2}
$$

## <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='polar.title' />

<Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='polar.intro' block />

$$
z = r(cos\theta + i\, sin\theta)
$$

<Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='polar.where' block />

- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='polar.modulus' /> $r = \lvert z \rvert = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$

- <Translate page='guides/ma-178/kompleks' key='polar.argument' /> $\theta = arg(z)$
